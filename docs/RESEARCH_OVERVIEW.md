# Research Overview

IBCA Lab (Identifiable Bio-Imaging & Clinical AI Lab) is a translational research wing of [CodeEX](https://codeex.space/). It works at the intersection of applied mathematics, biological imaging, identifiable representation learning, clinical AI and scientific software engineering.

## Principles

| Principle | Commitment |
|---|---|
| Mathematical rigour | Formulate the problem before choosing an architecture. |
| Identifiability | Prefer representations with distinguishable, meaningful factors. |
| Experimental integrity | Prevent leakage; evaluate generalisation honestly. |
| Reproducibility | Make methods, configs and evaluation inspectable. |
| Open science | Share reusable software and outputs responsibly. |

## Current state

The lab is establishing its foundations. There are no published results, benchmarks, deployed services or clinical validations. Planned work is described in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md); the three tracks are in [`RESEARCH_TRACKS.md`](RESEARCH_TRACKS.md).
