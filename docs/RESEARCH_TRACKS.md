# Research Tracks

The lab operates through exactly three tracks. Directory names match `ibca_core.TRACKS`.

## Track 01 — Cellular & Drug-Discovery Microscopy (`cellular-microscopy`)

Cell Painting / JUMP-CP. Separate biological signal (toxicity, Mechanism of Action) from batch effects, staining variation and imaging artefacts.
Planned methods: representation learning, disentangled/identifiable modelling, contrastive and self-supervised learning, batch-aware evaluation.
Intended deliverables: `ibca_core`, batch-robust evaluation protocols, reproducible pipelines, manuscripts.

## Track 02 — Real-Time Clinical Screening & Diagnostic Services (`clinical-ai`)

Efficient, uncertainty-aware clinical AI for constrained hardware.
Planned methods: lightweight vision models, quantisation and ONNX export, calibration, clinical text processing, local-first architecture.
Intended deliverables: self-hosted research applications, quantised artefacts, calibrated decision-support pipelines.
All outputs are research and decision-support only; see [`CLINICAL_SAFETY.md`](CLINICAL_SAFETY.md).

## Track 03 — Quantitative Bio-Imaging & Dynamical Systems (`quantitative-bioimaging`)

Physics-informed reconstruction, inverse problems, differential-equation modelling, numerical stability and uncertainty.
Intended deliverables: quantitative imaging toolkits, simulation benchmarks, modelling implementations, publications.

All deliverables listed above are **intended**; none are claimed as complete.
