# Clinical Safety

Nothing in this repository is a medical device or a clinically validated diagnostic system.

- Clinical applications are research and decision-support prototypes unless and until independent validation and applicable regulatory requirements are met.
- Do not use any output for patient care.
- Communicate limitations, uncertainty and failure modes alongside any model.
- Distinguish performance on a dataset from evidence of generalisation to independent data.
- Do not describe experimental models as validated or approved.
