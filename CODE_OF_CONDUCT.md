# Code of Conduct

## Standards

Participants must be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment,
discrimination, deliberate disruption, and misuse of health data are prohibited.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking fair,
appropriate action for unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This applies to project spaces and public activities representing the lab.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to saipradyumnat@codeex.space. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and confidentially where feasible.
